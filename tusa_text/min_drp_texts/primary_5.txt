I have always had a way with animals. Dogs like me. Cats like me. Birds like me, too. Mostly birds. For some reason. I have never known why. But birds really do like me. Take pigeons. Some friends wanted me to help them to keep pigeons. So i did. We built a house for them. Then we bought some pigeons. That is how we got started. We had a lot of fun with the pigeons. The boys would come up to the roof. We would eat and talk. We had a pool table on the roof, too. So we would play pool. And watch the pigeons fly around. Some people asked us why we played on the roof. We told them: "It is safer on the roof than in the street. Isn't it?" They did not say much after that. They would leave us alone. Taking care of the pigeons was a way to stay out of trouble. You feel good, too. So me and my birds worked out okay. I have learned a lot about life from them. You know birds do not care about one another's color. That is one thing i have learned from them. Seems like sometimes birds can be smarter than people. They get along, too. They play with each other. They have a good time. If they fight, they fight. They do not hold grudges. After they fight, whoever wins, they forget about it. It is over. That is a good thing for people to learn. I will tell you. I could watch my birds all day. My birds and me.
