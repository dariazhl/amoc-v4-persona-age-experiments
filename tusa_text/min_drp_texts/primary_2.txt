My day begins at six. The clock wakes me up. The cat meows. Little Maria gets up. The cat wants to eat. I open a can of cat food. The can opener will not work. I get our food. Maria drops her food. The cat eats Maria's food. Maria hits the cat. The cat bites Maria. Maria jumps away. She yells. Father and Mother get up. Maria and i use a toothbrush to clean our teeth. Maria drops her toothbrush on the floor. Father sits down to eat. The milk falls on the floor. Maria laughs and Mother yells. At seven we get ready for school. Everyone is in a hurry. Maria falls down the stairs. I cannot find my shoes. At eight i get on the bus. Surprise! It is the wrong bus. I get on the right bus. A little boy hits a little girl. A fight starts. I try to stop the fight. The boy and the girl hit me. The fight stops. I am glad! We get to school. At nine the bell rings. Everyone heads for the door. The door breaks. I go to my class. The work begins. My teacher leaves the room. Everyone talks. Pens and paper fly around the room. The teacher comes back. It is soon time for lunch. We eat in school. After lunch we play ball. I get hit in the face with a ball. I get hit in the back with a ball. The bell rings. Everyone runs to the door. The door breaks again. At three school is out.
