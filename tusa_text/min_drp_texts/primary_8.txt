It is morning. The town is covered by clouds and fog. It is a beautiful town. Volos is the name of the town. It is on the sea. Many big ships come to Volos. Milros and Nic are brothers. They are going to work on one of the ships. Their mother asked them to do this. Their family needed money. So what else could they do? The ship was blowing its horn. It was time for the brothers to go. They got on the ship. Many days passed. Milros and Nic worked hard. They did not get much sleep. At night they would sit together. They would talk about their town, Volos. They talked of the gardens in Volos. Everyone had flowers. The yards were all bright. Some houses were big. Some were small. All of them were beautiful. Every day you would see bicycles, donkeys and cars on the street. The streets were very clean. The people were always friendly. Milros and Nic missed the town. But they would be back home soon. In Turkish, kapuji means a door keeper. He buys bread and fruits. He throws out the trash. He cleans the garden. And lots of other things. In the winter, he works the heat. We live in a big building. Our Kapuji works for everyone in the building. Our Kapuji's name is Hikmet. He can read. He can write, too. Sometimes he is very happy. He is like a child. But sometimes he is not so happy. I guess because he works hard. Our Kapuji is our alarm clock.
