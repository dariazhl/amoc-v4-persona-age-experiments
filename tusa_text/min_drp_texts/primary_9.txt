There is no school today. I can stay in bed longer. My name is Billy. This is the room i share with my brother, Dan. Dan is only five. I have three sisters. I like them, but there is something nice about having a brother. We get up early. There is so much to do. For me, breakfast is the best meal of the day. Everything in the kitchen smells good after a long sleep. So many of the things we eat come from the farm. We have lots of milk and eggs and butter. There is a big garden around back. We grow corn and other vegetables. Mom bakes her own bread and cakes, too. There are not many foods that we need from the store. The more food i eat from the farm, the better i like it. After breakfast i help my father milk the cows. At first i had a hard time milking them. Now it is easy. Each cow on our farm has a name. It is hard to believe, but every cow is different. I like Sandy the best. When Sandy sees me, she comes right up to me and waits for a pat on the head. When the cows are milked, they are let out. They eat grass until late in the afternoon. When i am done with the cows, i can feed the birds. I made this bird house all by myself. I have my own set of tools in my room. This is the fifth bird house i have made. One nice thing about living on a farm is the birds you see.
